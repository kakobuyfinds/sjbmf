Frequently asked questions

What is this PikoBuy spreadsheet?

It is an independent shortlist of CAFANS product listings. Use it to compare items and keep track of the original CAFANS pages while planning a purchase through your preferred shopping agent.

Can I order every listing through PikoBuy?

Availability depends on whether PikoBuy accepts the original seller link and whether the seller still offers your variant. Verify both before placing an order.

What does the CSV include?

The CSV includes the titles, categories, estimated USD item prices, and CAFANS URLs for the products you saved. You can add size, color, and shipping quotes in your spreadsheet app.
